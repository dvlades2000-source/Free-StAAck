Why is everything actually free?

Short answer: most great tools have generous free tiers because they're trying to win you over. We just collect them in one place — and earn a small commission only when you upgrade later (which keeps the lights on at $0 cost to you).

Every tool has a real, usable free tier — not just a 7-day trial. Some offer paid upgrades, but the free version is enough for most people.

Some links are affiliate links. If you eventually upgrade to a paid plan, we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We never get paid to rank a tool higher.
